What happens to an LLM after it joins a price catalogue?

Rebuilt every day from the full git history of LiteLLM's model price file. Of the $models models added since $since, these are the estimated shares whose first change was each of the following.

Not financial or procurement advice. The numbers describe edits to a public catalogue, not what any model costs you. Disclaimer.

The maker and the grouped names come from naming rules (for example, eu.anthropic.claude-3-5-sonnet-20241022-v2:0 and snowflake/claude-3-5-sonnet are both grouped as anthropic / claude-3.5-sonnet). The host is LiteLLM's own provider field.

Changed in the last 7 days

Every price change, retirement date, removal and addition in the catalogue in the 7 days before the latest data. A single clean up can remove hundreds of rows at once.

Most repriced

Counts changes from one price to another. A price appearing for the first time is not counted.

Hosts ranked by chance of a price change within 1 year

Only hosts with at least 100 models still followed at one year are ranked; smaller hosts would give unreliable numbers.
