How this worksin four lines

Cost is your prompt and answer in tokens times each model's listed price. A token is about 4 characters of English, so costs are estimates.

Best value is the cheapest model whose Arena rating cannot be told apart from the top model's: people compared their answers and voted.

The task is guessed from your words, in your browser. Your prompt is never sent or saved.

The badge is the chance that model's price changes in the next 90 days, from the history of every change in the catalogue.

$sensitivity
Every change, or real changes only?

Real changes only leaves out unit fixes (a price made 100 times larger or smaller) and price changes undone within 30 days, and treats a model removed in a bulk clean up (50 or more removed on the same day) as still followed until that day.

About

llmledger reads the full git history of LiteLLM's model price file every day, and the Arena leaderboard for quality. It estimates what a prompt costs, which model is the best value, and how likely each price is to change.

What the estimates leave out

Prompt tokens are counted at about 4 characters per token; each model counts its own way, often 10 to 30% differently. Thinking models also bill hidden reasoning as answer tokens. Caching, batch discounts and long prompt prices are left out. When LiteLLM has no per-image or per-second price, an image counts as about 1,000 tokens, a PDF page about 1,500, a second of audio about 32 and a second of video about 300. "How many times" is how often you send the prompt; in a chat, each message also re-sends the conversation so far.
